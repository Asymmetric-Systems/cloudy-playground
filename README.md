## A Garden's Tale

In morning light the flowers wake,
Their petals kissed by gentle dew,
Beneath the sun's warm embrace they take,
Their colors bright in every hue.

The roots run deep through fertile ground,
Where secrets of the earth reside,
While whispered winds make rustling sound,
Through leaves that dance with gentle pride.

From tiny seeds great beauty grows,
With patient care through sun and rain,
Each blossom tells what nature knows,
That life persists through joy and pain.
